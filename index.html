Kalo Concierge

Guest messaging research for small Greek hotels.

I’d rather be upfront about what this is: a research project, not a service. There’s nothing to buy here, and I’m not selling anything.

Who I am

I’m Konstantinos Sohos, based in Ano Glyfada, Athens. I’ve been studying how small hotels in Greece handle guest messages — WhatsApp, Viber, email — both before a guest arrives and while they’re staying.

Why

Most of it lands on reception staff’s time. I want to understand what that actually costs, in delays, in bookings that quietly didn’t happen, in staff evenings.

What I’m asking for

Fifteen minutes on a phone call. No pitch, no deck, no follow-up sequence.

The rough questions:

  • Which channels do your guests actually use?
  • What happens to messages that arrive at night, or when reception is busy?
  • Has a slow reply ever cost you a booking, or led to a bad review?
  • Which languages cause the most difficulty?
  • What would something handling this reliably need to do, for you to trust it with your guests?

What happens to your answers

I keep research notes, and your details appear only if you explicitly ask me to use them. I don’t sell them, and I don’t share them with anyone.

You can skip any question, or stop halfway.

No product

I don’t have one, and I don’t have pricing or a deck. I’m trying to find out what’s genuinely true in Greek hotels before deciding whether anything is worth building.

Opting out

Reply to any message with “unsubscribe” and I won’t write to you again.
